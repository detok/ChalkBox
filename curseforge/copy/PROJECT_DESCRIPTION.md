# ChalkBox

**One addon. Better information. Less clutter.**

ChalkBox brings the little tools you reach for every day into one customizable companion for **World of Warcraft: Forever**.

## Make every adventure easier

- **LootSense:** Know why an item matters—quests, professions, recipes, KEEP lists and disenchant estimates.
- **Discoveries:** Build your own loot journal. Explore recorded drops by creature, dungeon or zone, with outdoor loot locations and learned flight paths on the map.
- **AuctionSense:** Check observed asking prices, scan the market manually and follow price history inside the Auction House.
- **QuestSense:** Keep your quest tracker focused and use an optional navigation arrow.
- **MacroForge:** Build macros from abilities you've learned, preview them and choose when to install.
- **The Bar:** Keep XP, gold, bag space, durability and other stats in a slim, adjustable HUD.
- **Adventure extras:** Combat Meter, Adventure Journal, camping reminders, Emota and optional social filters.

**Use what you like. Hide what you don't.** ChalkBox complements Blizzard's windows instead of filling the screen with more of them.

## Get started

Install **ChalkBox 2.2.26** for WoW Forever, then type **`/chalk`** to open settings. Your previous preferences and recorded history stay in SavedVariables when you upgrade.
