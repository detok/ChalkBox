# ChalkBox 2.2.28-quest-beta — QuestSense Priority & Nearby Improvements

**GITHUB BETA — not the stable CurseForge release.** Inventory Intelligence remains disabled by default, never posts trades, and adds Skill Up / Make Gold advice only if enabled. Requires live Forever testing.

- **Discoveries:** View recorded outdoor loot locations and learned flight paths on the local World Map. Browse loot history by area and creature.
- **LootSense:** Optional future-quest hints for selected items, alongside quest and profession context.
- **AuctionSense:** Historical asking-price charts and connected item-value insights.
- **The Bar:** Compact Blizzard icons on each stat (toggle in Appearance or `/chalk bar icons on|off`), clearer two-column colored hover details, adjustable module widths and remembered bank value. The Bar stays 18px high with all Mac notch positioning unchanged.
- **Combat Meter:** Ranked PARTY/SAY reports and compact display controls.
- **QuestSense Smart/Nearby:** Choose Closest First or Lowest Level First; completed turn-ins are last by default. Find accepted local objectives on sibling micro-maps and quest-specific waypoints; prefer newly accepted eligible quests. Choose 5 or 10 total watch slots, with manual overflow protected. Solo excludes verified elite/dungeon/raid quests. Native Blizzard tracker methods remain untouched.
- **QuestSense & MacroForge:** Follow a selected map waypoint; use MacroForge alongside Blizzard's macro editor.
- **Emota:** Optional Hunter pet interactions when the client supports them.

Built for World of Warcraft: Forever. Player preferences and recorded data persist across upgrades.
