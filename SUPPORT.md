## Report something

Use the [guided GitHub issue forms](https://github.com/detok/ChalkBox/issues/new/choose):

- **Bug report:** a reproducible failure or Lua error.
- **Compatibility:** conflicts involving another addon, client changes or UI scale.
- **Help:** installation, configuration and how-to questions.
- **Feature request:** a specific player workflow that could be improved.

Include the installed version from `/chalk update`, your Forever build (if available), and what happened. A redacted screenshot or short reproduction is useful.

For AuctionSense problems, include `/chalk auction status`, whether you clicked **Scan Market** or **Full listings**, and the status shown when it stopped. Don't keep retrying Full listings during the roughly 15-minute cooldown.

## Privacy and expectations

Never share passwords, tokens, account IDs, private chat or someone else's personal information in public issues. Keep your SavedVariables when upgrading; back them up before troubleshooting.

ChalkBox is maintained as a community addon. Reports are reviewed when maintainers are available; no guaranteed support response time is promised.

For sensitive security issues, follow [responsible disclosure](SECURITY.md) instead of posting exploit details publicly. [Community expectations](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) apply to the issue tracker.
