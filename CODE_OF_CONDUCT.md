# Community expectations

ChalkBox is a community project for improving World of Warcraft adventures.

Be constructive and respectful in issues, PRs and reviews. Focus criticism on behavior and code, not people. Don't harass contributors, post hateful content, disclose anyone's private information, or submit spam.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive submissions and restrict repeat disruptive behavior. For sensitive concerns, request a private contact route without publishing personal information.

These expectations apply to the public repository, not to any game client or Blizzard services.
